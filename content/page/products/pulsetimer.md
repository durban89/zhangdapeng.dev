A sleek, minimalist countdown timer and alarm for focused work, quick sessions, and everyday browser reminders.

PulseTimer is designed for people who want a simple timer without opening another application or dealing with unnecessary complexity.

**Set a countdown. Start a session. Get back to what you are doing.**

---

## Built for simplicity

Timers should not require complicated workflows.

PulseTimer keeps the experience focused on the essentials: set a duration, start the timer, and receive a reminder when the time is up.

Whether you are working on a short task, taking a break, preparing for a meeting, or simply need a quick reminder, PulseTimer stays out of your way.

---

## Core features

**Countdown timer**

Start a countdown in seconds and keep the timer available while you work.

**Alarms**

Set reminders for tasks, breaks, meetings, or anything else that needs your attention.

**Quick sessions**

Create short countdowns without configuring complicated workflows or settings.

**Minimal interface**

PulseTimer keeps the interface focused on the timer itself.

No unnecessary dashboards, accounts, or complicated configuration.

**Lightweight**

Designed to be fast and lightweight, so it stays out of your way while you work.

---

## Privacy by design

PulseTimer is designed with privacy in mind.

The extension does not require an account and does not need to send your timer data to a remote server.

Your timer experience stays local to your browser.

> No account. No unnecessary data collection. Just a simple timer.

## A simple workflow

Using PulseTimer takes only a few seconds:

1. Set the duration.
2. Start the countdown.
3. Continue with your task.
4. Get notified when the timer finishes.

No complicated setup. No account required. No unnecessary workflow.
