A privacy-first batch image resizer and icon generator for indie hackers, developers, and creators.

IndieResizer makes it easy to **resize, crop, and generate multiple image sizes** directly in your browser.

No uploads. No external servers. No complicated image-processing workflow.

---

## Built for developers

Preparing images for different projects often means repeating the same resize and export operations over and over.

IndieResizer is designed to make that workflow faster and simpler.

Whether you are preparing assets for a browser extension, a web application, a mobile app, or a new SaaS product, you can process your images directly in the browser without sending them to a remote service.

---

## Core features

**Batch image resizing**

Process multiple images in a single workflow instead of resizing files individually.

**Multiple output sizes**

Generate the image sizes you need for different projects, platforms, and development workflows.

**Image cropping**

Crop images before resizing to create cleaner and more consistent assets.

**Icon generation**

Generate multiple icon sizes from a source image for browser extensions, applications, developer tools, and digital products.

**Local processing**

Images are processed directly in your browser.

Your source files do not need to leave your device.

---

## Privacy by design

Privacy is a core part of IndieResizer.

The extension does not require you to upload your images to a remote server. Image processing happens locally in the browser, keeping your source files on your device.

This makes IndieResizer a practical choice for working with:

- Private assets
- Unpublished product designs
- Application icons
- Website assets
- Internal project materials
- Client or proprietary images

> Your images stay on your device while you work.

## A simple workflow

The workflow is intentionally straightforward:

1. Select your images.
2. Choose the required output sizes.
3. Crop or resize when needed.
4. Generate the output files.
5. Continue building your project.

No complicated configuration. No upload step. No external image-processing service.
